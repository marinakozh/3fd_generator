EoS data for using in hydrodynamic codes
(for crossover and 1-order PT EoS's)
[A. S. Khvorostukin, V. V. Skokov, V. D. Toneev and
K. Redlich, Eur. Phys. J. C 48, 531 (2006)].


In all programs, the energy density and pressure are always used in units (n0*mN).
Here, mN is the nucleon mass (mN=938.9MeV),
n0 is the normal nuclear density, n0=0.15fm-3.


The energy density and pressure are represented as a sum of compressional energy density (pressure) and thermal ones: 

e(n,T) = e(n,T=0) + eT(n,T) 
P(n,T) = P(n,T=0) + PT(n,T)

The following data tables should be prepared:


1. Compressional energy density ec and pressure Pc are tabulated in the range
[xmin,xmax], x=n/n0, at the points:
 xj=xmin+(xmax-xmin)*(j-1)/(Nx0-1), j=1,2,...,Nx0
 xmax/(Nx0-1)=0.1,
 Xmin0  = 0.01.

Compressional energy density and pressure are stored in arrays ecmp(j), pcmp(j), j=1,...,Nx0.


2. Thermal energy density eT(T,n) and pressure PT(T,n) are calculated on grid [Ti,xj], where xj is defined above, and the temperature (in MeV) Ti=Tmax*(i-1)/(Nt0-1), i=1,2,...,Nt0.
Tmax/(Nt0-1)=1 MeV



Thermal energy density and pressure are stored in arrays enth(i,j), prth(i,j): 
the first index corresponds to the temperature, and the second one, to the baryon density. 

Arrays ecmp(j), pcmp(j), enth(i,j), prth(i,j) are stored in the file in the following format (FORTRAN notation):
*-------------------------------------
      character*50 fname
	  ...
      open (unit=nunit,status='old',file=fname)
      read(nunit,*)(ecmp(j),j=1,Nx0)
      read(nunit,*)(pcmp(j),j=1,Nx0)
      do i=1,Nt0
       read(nunit,*)(enth(i,j),j=1,Nx0)
      enddo
      do i=1,Nt0
       read(nunit,*)(prth(i,j),j=1,Nx0)
      enddo
      close (nunit)
*-------------------------------------
These arrays with Tmax=800MeV and xmax=60 are stored in the files for 1PT EoS and crossover EoS, respectively,
$HOME/NFD/rhiclib/EoS/2phaseEoS/enpr800x60.dat,
$HOME/NFD/rhiclib/EoS/mixEoS/enpr800x60.dat.



3. Thermal energy density and pressure of baryon-free matter (i.e. at n=0) are stored in the arrays yth(i), pth(i), i=2,...,Nt0. 
They are storred in file in the following format (FORTRAN notation):
*---------------------------------
      character*50 fnameb0
	  ...
      open (unit=nunit,status='old',file=fnameb0)
      read(nunit,*)(yth(i),i=2,Nt0)
      read(nunit,*)(pth(i),i=2,Nt0)
      close (nunit)
*---------------------------------
These arrays with Tmax=800MeV are stored in the files for 1PT EoS and crossover EoS, respectively,
$HOME/NFD/rhiclib/EoS/2phaseEoS/epn800b0.dat,
$HOME/NFD/rhiclib/EoS/mixEoS/epn800b0.dat.



4. Fraction of QGP (valued from 0 to 1) is stored in the arrays wpl0(i) and wpl(i,j) for baryon-free matter (nB=0) and baryon-rich matter, respectively. 
They are storred in file in the following format (FORTRAN notation):
*---------------------------------
      character*50 wplname
	  ...
      open (unit=nunit,status='old',file=wplname)
c     *      file='../EoS/2phaseEoS/wpl800x60.dat')
       wpl0(1)=0.
       read (nunit,*)(wpl0(i),i=2,Nt0)
	 do i=1,Nt0
	  read(nunit,*)(wpl(i,j),j=1,Nx0)
       enddo
	close (nunit)
*---------------------------------
These arrays are stored in the files for 1PT EoS and crossover EoS, respectively,
$HOME/NFD/rhiclib/EoS/2phaseEoS/wpl800x60.dat,
$HOME/NFD/rhiclib/EoS/mixEoS/wpl800x60.dat.




5. Baryonic chemical potential muB [in MeV], calculated on the same [Ti,xj] gird, are stored in the array umbar(i,j). 
Array umbar(i,j) is stored in separate file in the following format (FORTRAN notation):
*---------------------------------
     character*(*)  fumbar
     ... 
     open (unit=nunit,status='old',file=fumbar)
     do i=1,Nt0
       read(nunit,*)(umbar(i,j),j=1,Nx0)
     enddo
     close (unit=nunit)
*---------------------------------
These arrays with Tmax=800MeV and xmax=60 are stored in the files for 1PT EoS and crossover EoS, respectively,
$HOME/NFD/rhiclib/EoS/2phaseEoS/800x60/umbar.dat,
$HOME/NFD/rhiclib/EoS/mixEoS/800x60/umbar.dat.



6. Strange chemical potential mus [in MeV], calculated on the same [Ti,xj] gird, is stored in the array umstr(i,j). 
Array umstr(i,j) is stored in separate file umstr.dat in the following format (FORTRAN notation):
*---------------------------------
     character*(*)  fumstr
     ... 
     open (unit=nunit,status='old',file=fumstr)
      do i=1,Nt0
       read(nunit,*)(umstr(i,j),j=1,Nx0)
      enddo
     close (unit=nunit)
*---------------------------------
These arrays with Tmax=800MeV and xmax=60 are stored in the files for 1PT EoS and crossover EoS, respectively,
$HOME/NFD/rhiclib/EoS/2phaseEoS/800x60/umstr.dat,
$HOME/NFD/rhiclib/EoS/mixEoS/800x60/umstr.dat.



7. In general case, it's assumed that nuclear matter consists of baryons, mesons, quark and gluons. 
For each type of constituent particles, the scalar density and proper density (zero component of the current in the rest frame) 
is tabulated on the same grid [Ti,xj] and stored in separate files. 
The following correspondence of densities, arrays and files takes place: 
array	         density	            file name
srQrk(i,j)	scalar density of quarks	srQrk.dat
srmes(i,j)	scalar density of mesons	srmes.dat
srGlu(i,j)	scalar density of gluons	srGlu.dat
srbar(i,j)	scalar density of baryons	srbar.dat
rrQrk(i,j)	proper density of quarks	rrQrk.dat
rrmes(i,j)	proper density of mesons	rrmes.dat
rrGlu(i,j)	proper density of gluons	rrGlu.dat
rrbar(i,j)	proper density of baryons	rrbar.dat

All densities are  stored in files in units n0 (normal nuclear density) in the following format (FORTRAN notation, example for srmes):
*---------------------------------
     character*(*)  flsrmes
     ... 
     open (unit=nunit,status='old',file=flsrmes)
      do i=1,Nt0
       read(nunit,*)(srmes(i,j),j=1,Nx0)
      enddo
     close (unit=nunit)
*---------------------------------
All chemical potential and density files are located in one directory.
